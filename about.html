About Mansa-Multi-specialty-Clinic

Mansa-Super-specialty-Clinic(मनसा क्लिनिक) is a family clinic at 02, Usha Colony, Beniwal Chauraha, Loha Mandi, Jaipur. Families from Loha Mandi, Usha Colony and nearby colonies come to us for daily OPD, laboratory tests, child vaccination and medicines.

Our consultants come from SMS Medical College, Safdarjung Hospital, J.K. Loan Hospital and Fortis — so you get teaching-hospital quality advice close to home.

Our mission

Expert care and compassionate treatment for a healthier tomorrow — your health is our priority.

What we stand for

Comprehensive care

Physician, gynaecology, paediatrics, skin and dental OPD in one neighbourhood clinic.

Accurate diagnosis

In-house laboratory for blood, urine, sugar, thyroid, vitamin, dengue and malaria testing.

Personalised treatment

Senior consultants from SMS, Safdarjung and Fortis backgrounds explain every prescription.

Everything in one visit

Consultation, lab sample, ECG, vaccination and pharmacy — without travelling across the city.
